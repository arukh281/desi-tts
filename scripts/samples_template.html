desi-tts samples

I fine-tuned XTTS-v2 on 24 minutes of my own voice so it speaks Indian English, Hindi and Hinglish. Here is how it sounds next to the model I started from.

Baseline is pretrained XTTS-v2 copying my voice from three short reference clips, with my text normaliser. Final is my fine-tuned model (E3, step 1500) with the same normaliser, and with Hinglish sent in Devanagari. Every clip is synthetic, one take with seed 1234, and I didn't pick them for sounding good. CER is Whisper large-v3's character error rate on that one clip, so lower is better. Headphones help.

The Hinglish fix

Pretrained XTTS reads Roman-script Hinglish as if it were English and garbles it. Fine-tuning helps a bit. Sending the same sentence in Devanagari, converted automatically with IndicXlit, fixes most of it. The Hinglish CER here is rough: Hindi Whisper against AI-drafted Devanagari references that I checked.

Where it still fails: names
