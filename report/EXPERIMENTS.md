# Experiment table

CER = Whisper large-v3 character error rate on the 88-sentence benchmark (lower is better). Hinglish CER is a rough score: Hindi Whisper against my hand-written Devanagari. "Sim" = ECAPA speaker similarity to my reference clip in the same language. Single-seed numbers come from one run (seed 1234); 3-seed numbers average seeds 1-3 with a paired bootstrap 95% interval over sentences. A crashed sentence counts as CER 1.0. Every number links to a file in [`results/`](../results).

| # | Experiment | Change | Result | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E0 | Pretrained XTTS-v2, raw text | Baseline: my 3 reference clips, no text processing | 8 of 12 Hindi rows crash (XTTS's digit expander fails on Hindi). CER en 0.115, hi 0.960, Hinglish 0.485 ([single_seed](../results/single_seed.md)) | Raw text isn't usable. A text normaliser is required before anything else. |
| E1 | Pretrained + normaliser | Numbers, ₹, dates, times, acronyms written out as spoken | No crashes. 3 seeds: CER all 0.125, en 0.037, hi 0.198, Hinglish 0.400; sim en 0.502 ([summary](../results/summary.md)) | The baseline every fine-tuning claim is measured against. Hinglish is the worst part. |
| E2 | Fine-tune on session 1 (16 min), pick checkpoint by val CER | 1000 steps; lowest val CER on 8 en+hi clips | Picked step 250. Sim en 0.718 vs E1 0.497; CER Hinglish 0.311 vs 0.431 ([single_seed](../results/single_seed.md), [selection](../results/runs/E2/select/selection.csv)). I listened: robotic, not my voice | **Failed.** The pick was barely trained; CER on 8 clips is too noisy to choose by. Changed the rule to lowest val loss with CER and duration guards. Without the normaliser it also read raw digits worse than E0. |
| E2b | Session 1 only, E3's recipe | Same 2000 steps and selection rule as E3, less data (208 vs 316 clips) | Both runs picked step 1000; val loss rises after it ([run 1](../results/runs/E2b_run1/select/selection.csv), [run 2](../results/runs/E2b_run2/select/selection.csv)). 3 seeds vs E3: E3 − E2b overall +0.002 [−0.016, +0.022]; en +0.008 [−0.015, +0.034]; hi +0.035 [−0.003, +0.094]; Hinglish −0.045 [−0.076, −0.017] ([bootstrap](../results/bootstrap_E2b_vs_E3.txt)). Sim en 0.721 vs 0.730. Run 1, single seed: CER en 0.028, hi 0.062, Hinglish 0.263 ([single_seed](../results/single_seed.md)) | The extra 8 minutes (mostly Hindi and Hinglish) only measurably helped Hinglish. English and Hindi CER can't tell 16 and 24 minutes apart. E2's bad result was the checkpoint pick, not the data. Run 2 didn't reproduce run 1 bit for bit (same pick, val loss within 0.002). |
| E3 | Fine-tune on sessions 1+2 (24 min) | 316 train / 17 val clips, 2000 steps, val-loss rule | Picked step 1500 (2000 failed the CER guard). Run 2 reproduced run 1's selection table exactly. 3 seeds: CER all 0.084 vs E1 0.125, −0.041 [−0.083, −0.009]; Hinglish 0.222 vs 0.400; en and hi within noise; sim en 0.730 ([bootstrap](../results/bootstrap_E1_vs_E3.txt)) | Fine-tuning moves the voice a lot and helps Hinglish. Listeners preferred it for voice match 15 of 16. Names got worse (0.107 → 0.133). |
| P7 | Hinglish via Devanagari at inference (the failure fix) | IndicXlit + a small lexicon turn Roman Hinglish into Devanagari, sent to XTTS as Hindi | On E3: Hinglish CER 0.222 → 0.098, −0.124 [−0.177, −0.068]; held-out 10 sentences 0.205 → 0.119, −0.086 [−0.146, −0.034] ([bench](../results/bootstrap_E3_vs_E3_deva.txt), [held-out](../results/bootstrap_E3_heldout_en_vs_E3_heldout_deva.txt)). Listeners chose it 4 of 4 | **Kept.** The route gives most of the intelligibility (pretrained + route is 0.097); fine-tuning gives the voice. |
| E4 | Train Hinglish as Devanagari | Same audio, Hinglish transcripts transliterated and tagged Hindi | Held-out Hinglish 0.126 vs E3 + route 0.119, +0.007 [−0.016, +0.032] ([bootstrap](../results/bootstrap_E3_heldout_deva_vs_E4.txt)) | No gain, and IndicXlit mistakes go into the training text. Dropped. |
| E5 | Sampling sweep | Temperature 0.75/0.5/0.3 × top-p 0.85/0.7, 3 seeds, val + held-out only | Val CER 0.019-0.028, held-out Hinglish 0.107-0.123, no setting best on both ([sweep](../results/runs/E5_sweep_long/sweep.csv)) | Within noise. Kept the defaults (0.75 / 0.85). |
| E6 | Split long sentences | Split above 120 characters, 0.25 s gap; 5 new long sentences | CER en+hi 0.0088 unsplit vs 0.0175 split; Hinglish 0.140 vs 0.143 ([long](../results/runs/E5_sweep_long/long.csv)) | Doesn't help. Off. |
| Final | E3 + normaliser + Devanagari route | Default sampling, no splitting | 3 seeds: CER all 0.061 vs E1 0.125, −0.064 [−0.110, −0.025]; Hinglish 0.098 vs 0.400, −0.303 [−0.392, −0.215]; en and hi within noise ([bootstrap](../results/bootstrap_E1_vs_Final.txt)) | The shipped system. Main remaining weakness: names. |
