desi-tts samples

An Indian English, Hindi and Hinglish voice made by fine-tuning XTTS-v2 on 24 minutes of my own recordings.

Baseline: pretrained XTTS-v2 cloning my voice from three short reference clips, with my text normaliser. Final: the fine-tuned model (E3, step 1500) with the same normaliser, and Hinglish sent as Devanagari. All clips are synthetic, one take each with seed 1234, not picked for being good. CER is Whisper large-v3's character error rate on that one clip (lower is better). Use headphones if you can.

The Hinglish fix

Pretrained XTTS reads Roman-script Hinglish as English and garbles it. Fine-tuning helps a bit; sending the same sentence in Devanagari (transliterated automatically with IndicXlit) fixes most of it. Hinglish CER here is a rough score: Hindi Whisper against my hand-written Devanagari.

b061 · hinglish · conversational

Aapka payment successfully receive ho gaya hai.

Baseline (Roman)CER 0.38
Fine-tuned, RomanCER 0.06
Final (Devanagari)CER 0.06

b063 · hinglish · dates times

Where it still fails: names

Indian names and places are the main remaining weakness. On these the baseline is often better.

b009 · en · names

Hello, is this Venkatesh Iyer speaking?

Final model babbles: it adds a sentence that isn't there (Whisper: "...Venkatesh here speaking? My parents would sound quite saluted speaking.").

FinalCER 1.25
