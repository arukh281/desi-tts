Which voice sounds right?

You'll hear 22 pairs of clips. Each pair is the same sentence said by two versions of a text-to-speech voice built from Aradhya's recordings. Use headphones if you can. About 10 minutes.

For each pair, play A and B, then answer three quick questions. Go with your first impression. There are no right answers, and some pairs may sound very similar.

Sharing a phone? Each person starts fresh: tap "Start fresh" (or change the name) so the last person's answers are cleared.

Send your answers

When you've done the pairs, tap Copy and paste the text into WhatsApp to Aradhya.
